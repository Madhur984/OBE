The marks

One row per student, one column per CO, with the maximum marks for each CO. Write AB or DB where a student did not sit the exam — they come out of that CO's denominator rather than counting as a zero.

Choose your marks workbook, or drop it here .xlsx — the department's own CO Attainment sheet works as it is

Copy the columns straight out of Excel — a header row of Name, CO1, CO2 …, then the maximum marks, then the students.

Targets

Both are yours to set. T1 is a mark — the share of the maximum a student must reach to have attained that outcome. T2 is a share of students — how many must cross T1 for the course to score the full 3.

attainment = min(3, crossing % ÷ T2 × 3)
