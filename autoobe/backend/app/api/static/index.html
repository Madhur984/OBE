Drop the AKTU semester PDF. Every course in it is read separately — code, title, L‑T‑P and its five course outcomes — with Bloom's levels taken from the K column where the syllabus declares one, and inferred from the opening verb where it does not.

Choose a syllabus, or drop one here .pdf · .docx · .txt — nothing leaves this machine

Your file always wins. Where a course has no syllabus in it — a lab block, or a paper typed in by hand — AKTU's own published syllabus stands in, and the matrix names the document and session it came from so you can refuse it.

What happens next

  1. Outcomes are read — every course in the file separately, with its code, title and L‑T‑P.
  2. Bloom's levels are set — taken from the K column where the syllabus declares one, inferred from the verb where it does not, and always labelled which.
  3. The CO × PO matrix is proposed — a competency of 0, 1 or 2 per cell, from the outcome text and the verbatim PO statements.
  4. Levels are derived — Rule 1, then Rule 2 for strong matches. You review; nothing is authored for you.
  5. The document downloads — matrix, PO averages and the Bloom's levels behind them, in Word, Excel, CSV or Markdown.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The file is read in this process and the arithmetic is deterministic — the same syllabus gives the same matrix next year.

    4 CO → PO / PSO matrix purple = proposed blue = your override

    The big number is the mapping level (0–3) that reaches the SAR. The small chip under it is the competency (0–2) it came from. Click any cell to see the rule that produced it, and to change the competency.

    Programme Specific Outcomes — your department writes these. AutoOBE will not invent them: a PSO with no statement has no column, because a column nobody can point to a statement for is not defensible in front of a peer team. Its PL is read from the leading verb of what you type, exactly as a PO's is.

    5 Programme outcome averages

    Two denominators, both shown, because they give different numbers and a peer team will ask which one you used. Avg (mapped) divides by the COs that actually map to that PO — the AKTU articulation-matrix convention, and the figure that goes into the SAR. Avg (all COs) divides by every CO in the course, counting a blank as 0. Nothing is rounded silently: the rounded whole number sits beside the mean it came from.

      Every download carries the matrix, these averages, and the Bloom's levels they were derived from.
